# Commands

## Obols

- `/balance` is provided by GardenEssentials and reads the GardenCore balance.
- `/obol balance`
- `/obol withdraw`
- `/obol deposit`
- `/obol admin`

## Claims and organizations

- `/claim`
- `/company`
- `/garden`

The player-facing property, rental, citizenship, and territory commands live in GardenLands.
